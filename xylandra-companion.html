ORACLE RESOLUTION
Roll 1 Chance Die (green) + 1 Risk Die (red).
Chance highest → YES  |  Risk highest → NO

Both low (1-3): add BUT
Both high (4-6): add AND

ADVANTAGE: roll 2 Chance + 1 Risk, keep highest Chance
DISADVANTAGE: roll 1 Chance + 2 Risk, keep highest Risk
TWIST COUNTER
Starts at 0. Every time both dice show the same number (doubles), add 1.
Counter below 3: treat result as Yes, But…
Counter reaches 3: a TWIST happens — roll on the Twist Table and reset to 0.
HARM & LUCK
All characters start with Luck 6.
In conflict, alternate turns. When you fail defence, take Harm. When you succeed, cause Harm.
When Luck reaches 0: face mortal danger — next attack ends the conflict.
Context determines outcome: captured, injured, fled, or dead.
CHARACTER TRAITS
Concept — adjective-noun pair describing the character
Skills (x2) — specific abilities, not common traits
Frailty — physical, mental, or social weakness
Gear (x2) — notable equipment (not everyday items)
Goal — long-term objective
Motive — what drives the goal
Nemesis — person or org that hinders the protagonist

At story end: add 1 new trait related to how it ended.
XYLANDRA SPECIAL RULES
Survival in Harsh Lands — Ask oracle for desert/jungle/sky travel. Yes+And = safe passage and bonus. No+And = lost, injured, or worse.

Relic Activation — Oracle determines if ancient tech functions. Failure may trigger guardian, alarm, or temporal anomaly.

Time Anomaly — Passing through fractured zones: Yes+And = glimpse future. No+And = displaced from time, memories lost.

Skyship Combat — Uses same Harm/Luck system. Ships have Luck traits. Frailties apply as disadvantages.
