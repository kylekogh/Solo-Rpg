RULES · STATUS CONDITIONS · SPACE COMBAT
COMBAT SEQUENCE
1 — TURN ORDER All combatants roll d10 + GRA. Highest goes first. Ties → highest base GRA stat wins.
2 — ENEMY MOVE Roll d10 on the enemy's move table. Apply result immediately.
3 — YOUR TURN Take 1 Main Action + 1 Side Action. May swap Main for second Side Action.
4 — REPEAT Continue until one side reaches 0 HP.
MAIN ACTIONS (choose 1 per turn)
ActionHow
WEAPONRoll base damage + relevant stat vs target's Armor
HACKROLL MIND vs enemy. Success: HACK fires. Fail: roll Malware table
CYBERTECHUse an active Cybertech ability (if it uses Main Action)
ESCAPEROLL GRACE vs enemy. Success: leave combat. Fail: remain in combat
SIDE ACTIONS
Use one item from inventory, or any ability tagged [Side]. Can be performed at any point during your turn — before or after Main Action. Some Cybertech and Hacks cost Side Actions instead of Main Actions.
ARMOR & DAMAGE
Armor ♥ reduces incoming damage by 1 per Armor point. You can have a maximum of 3 Armor (unless Experimental Cybertech specifies otherwise). Temporary Armor stacks on top of regular Armor but expires at the stated time.
HP & ENERGY RULES
Max HP = 20. Energy § = 0–20. Spend § on Hacks, Cybertech, some actions. If HP reaches 0: roll d6 on Abyssal Scar table (add +1 per scar already suffered). 6+ = character dies. Below 6 = survive with consequences — set HP/Hull/Fuel/Energy to 10, place starship on STAR hex. HP and Energy do NOT auto-restore between combats.
MULTIPLE ENEMIES
When facing multiple enemies, each takes a turn in initiative order. You may use Area Attacks (grenades, certain Hacks like Supernova/Blackout) to affect all enemies simultaneously. When multiple enemies have the same HP total, track them with a shared pool — subtract damage from whichever you hit.
BOARDING (Space Combat)
When your ship enters Critical Condition (Hull ≤ 10), an enemy may attempt to Board. The Quantum System ship module allows you to teleport 1 PC inside the enemy ship. Boarding initiates a regular ground combat encounter inside the enemy ship.
SOCIAL ROLLS
When interacting with NPCs, ROLL GRACE. Success means the NPC is receptive to your request. Use Social Rolls for: negotiating prices (−10–20% discount), convincing NPCs to share information, gaining Affinity with crewmembers, taming alien creatures (ISF Astrobiology ending). WHAT IS ASTROPRISMA?
ASTROPRISMA is a sci-fi roleplaying game for 1–5 players about exploring a vast retrofuturistic post-Collapse universe. Create your character, customize your spaceship, gather crewmembers, join factions, and carve your own story across a hex grid star system.

GRA — GraceAgility, stealth, precision, charm. Determines turn order in combat. Escape rolls.
MIN — MindIntelligence, perception, willpower. Required for casting Hacks.
TEC — TechEngineering, hacking, repairing, piloting. Ship systems and drones.

INVENTORY & MEMORY SLOTS
Inventory: 8 slots. Holds weapons, grenades, narcobotics, armor, items. Hacks are NOT stored here — they go in Memory Slots.

Memory Slots: 3 base slots (up to 6 with certain Cybertech). Store active Hacks here. Only Hacks in Memory Slots can be used in combat.

Cargo Hold: Ship storage. Items moved here at cycle end. Accessible in space combat, not ground fights.
NARCOBOTICS
Consumable drugs that grant temporary stat bonuses for 1 cycle. Each has an Overdose stage — if you use the same narcotic too many times in a row, negative effects apply instead. Wear off at end of cycle.
CONNECTIONS & SOCIAL
Affinity: Your relationship score with individual NPCs (0–5). Increased through positive interactions.

Favor: Your standing with each Faction (0–10♥). Complete missions and events to gain it.

Social Rolls: ROLL GRACE to negotiate, persuade, or charm NPCs. Success = they're receptive. Failure = they refuse or turn hostile.
HYPERDRIVE
2 charges per cycle. Spend a charge to: reroll any die, deal maximum damage on a weapon attack, or use a Hacks/Cybertech ability without paying its Energy cost. Charges do not carry over between cycles.
THE EXPLORATION CYCLE
STEP 1 — PLAN Declare your intended destination hex on the Star Map.
STEP 2 — ROLL EXPLORATION Roll d6 on your ring's table to get the encounter category. Roll a second d6 for the specific event.
STEP 3 — RESOLVE Play out the encounter — combat, NPC interaction, planet exploration, or a choice.
STEP 4 — MOVE SHIP Move your ship token to the destination hex. Spend 1 Fuel.
STEP 5 — END OF CYCLE Restore 3§ Energy if HP is at maximum. Narcobotics wear off. Advance time-tracked conditions. Push session log to journal.

CHALLENGE ROLLS — the core mechanic
Roll two d10s. Add your relevant stat to the PLAYER die. Add the opponent's relevant stat (or 0 for environment challenges) to the CHALLENGE die. If your total is strictly higher, you succeed. Equal = fail.
Example — Escape (ROLL GRACE): Your GRA is 3. Enemy GRA is 2.
You roll d10 [4] + 3 = 7 vs d10 [5] + 2 = 7 → FAIL (not strictly higher)
You roll d10 [5] + 3 = 8 vs d10 [4] + 2 = 6 → SUCCESS
Environment rolls (falling, heat, tech failures) have no opponent — the challenge die is just d10 with no stat added.
SOCIAL ROLLS
ROLL GRACE when interacting with NPCs. Success: they're receptive — negotiate prices (−10–20%), share intel, gain Affinity. Failure: they refuse or become hostile. Some encounters specify a different stat (ROLL MIND for deception, ROLL VIG for intimidation).
DIFFICULTY — affects LOOT, not the roll
Enemy difficulty (· Easy ·· Medium ··· Hard ···· Boss) determines what loot you roll for on defeat. It does not modify the challenge die. Every challenge roll is always d10+your stat vs d10+enemy stat.

ORACLE — asking yes/no questions
When you need to determine if something is true or what an NPC does, ask the Oracle a yes/no question and pick an odds level:

Likely — success on 4+. Even — success on 5+. Unlikely — success on 7+.

COMBAT SEQUENCE
1. INITIATIVE Everyone rolls d10 + GRA. Highest goes first. Ties: higher base GRA wins.
2. YOUR TURN Take 1 Main Action + 1 Side Action. Can swap Main for a second Side.
3. ATTACK ROLL stat vs enemy. Hit: roll weapon damage dice + stat − enemy Armor = HP lost.
4. USE A HACK ROLL MIND vs enemy. Success: Hack fires. Fail: roll Malware table (d10).
5. ENEMY TURN Roll d10 on enemy's move table. Apply result.
6. END TURN Tick status conditions down by 1. OVERHEAT deals damage.
7. DEFEAT Enemy at 0 HP: roll loot, gain XP. You at 0 HP: roll Abyssal Scar.
ACTIONS
Main Actions: Attack with a weapon, use a Hack, use Cybertech (if tagged Main), attempt to escape.

Side Actions: Use 1 inventory item, use a Hack/Cybertech tagged [Side], swap weapon mods.

Escape: ROLL GRACE vs highest-GRA enemy. Success: you leave combat. Fail: remain, enemy acts normally. Jet Propulsors make escape a Side Action.
DAMAGE & ARMOR
Roll weapon's base dice + relevant stat. Subtract enemy's Armor (♥). Remainder = HP lost. Minimum 0 — armor cannot heal enemies.

Armor max is 3♥ (unless Experimental Cybertech specifies otherwise).

MULTIPLE ENEMIES & HACKS
Multiple enemies: Each takes a turn in initiative order. Area attacks (grenades, Blackout, Kraken) affect all simultaneously.

Hacks: Stored in Memory Slots (3 base). ROLL MIND vs enemy to cast. Failure = roll Malware table. Each hack has an Energy § cost deducted on use.
SPACE COMBAT SEQUENCE
1. INITIATIVE Roll d10 + GRA for your ship. Highest goes first.
2. YOUR TURN Roll your Action Dice pool (1 die per Action). Assign each die to a Weapon or System module.
3. MODULES Each die that meets a module's trigger number fires its effect.
4. ENEMY TURN Roll d10 on the enemy ship's move table. Apply result.
5. SHIELDS Shields absorb incoming damage first. Regenerate at end of your turn.
6. VICTORY Enemy reaches 0 Hull. Roll loot.
SHIP STATS

Fuel: 1 per hex moved. Max 20.
Critical Condition: Hull ≤ 10. Ship SKILL activates. Enemy may attempt Boarding.
Shields: Block incoming damage. Destroyed before Hull takes hits.

MODULES
