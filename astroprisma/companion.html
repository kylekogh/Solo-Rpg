CHALLENGE ROLL
Roll two d10s. Add your relevant stat to the Player die. Add opponent stat to the Challenge die. Player die must be STRICTLY HIGHER to succeed.
COMBAT SEQUENCE
1 — TURN ORDER All combatants roll d10 + GRA. Highest goes first.
2 — ENEMY MOVE Roll d10 on the enemy's move table.
3 — YOUR TURN Take 1 Main Action + 1 Side Action.
4 — REPEAT Continue until one side reaches 0 HP.
MAIN ACTIONS
ActionHow
WEAPONRoll base damage + relevant stat vs target's Armor
HACKROLL MIND vs enemy. Success: fires. Fail: roll Malware
CYBERTECHUse an active Cybertech ability
ESCAPEROLL GRACE vs enemy. Success: leave. Fail: stay
HP & ENERGY RULES
Max HP = 20. Energy § = 0–20. If HP reaches 0: roll d6 on Abyssal Scar table. 6+ = character dies.
STATUS CONDITIONS
OVERHEAT
Cannot use Energy or fire guns. Recover by taking no actions on your next turn.
SILENCE
Cannot cast Hacks. Lasts 1 turn or until healed.
STUN
Skip your next turn.
