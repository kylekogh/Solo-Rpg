1 — TURN ORDER All combatants roll d10 + GRA. Highest goes first. Ties → highest base GRA stat wins.
2 — ENEMY MOVE Roll d10 on the enemy's move table. Apply result immediately.
3 — YOUR TURN Take 1 Main Action + 1 Side Action. May swap Main for second Side Action.
4 — REPEAT Continue until one side reaches 0 HP.

SIDE ACTIONS
Use one item from inventory, or any ability tagged [Side]. Can be performed at any point during your turn — before or after Main Action. Some Cybertech and Hacks cost Side Actions instead of Main Actions.
ARMOR & DAMAGE
Armor ♥ reduces incoming damage by 1 per Armor point. You can have a maximum of 3 Armor (unless Experimental Cybertech specifies otherwise). Temporary Armor stacks on top of regular Armor but expires at the stated time.
HP & ENERGY RULES
Max HP = 20. Energy § = 0–20. Spend § on Hacks, Cybertech, some actions. If HP reaches 0: roll d6 on Abyssal Scar table (add +1 per scar already suffered). 6+ = character dies. Below 6 = survive with consequences — set HP/Hull/Fuel/Energy to 10, place starship on STAR hex. HP and Energy do NOT auto-restore between combats.
MULTIPLE ENEMIES
When facing multiple enemies, each takes a turn in initiative order. You may use Area Attacks (grenades, certain Hacks like Supernova/Blackout) to affect all enemies simultaneously. When multiple enemies have the same HP total, track them with a shared pool — subtract damage from whichever you hit.
BOARDING (Space Combat)
When your ship enters Critical Condition (Hull ≤ 10), an enemy may attempt to Board. The Quantum System ship module allows you to teleport 1 PC inside the enemy ship. Boarding initiates a regular ground combat encounter inside the enemy ship.
SOCIAL ROLLS
When interacting with NPCs, ROLL GRACE. Success means the NPC is receptive to your request. Use Social Rolls for: negotiating prices (−10–20% discount), convincing NPCs to share information, gaining Affinity with crewmembers, taming alien creatures (ISF Astrobiology ending). Failure = they refuse or become hostile.

TURF WAR — COUNTING CONTROL
Before advancing to a new system, count the number of Settlements and zones of control of each faction in the current map. The faction with the highest total takes over the Base of the faction with the lowest amount of territory under their control.
ZONES OF CONTROL
As factions gain Strength they acquire territory — captured tiles called zones of control. Zones can only be accessed by characters with 0 or higher Favor with the ruling faction. Negative Favor = attacked on sight. Roll d6 on the Faction Troops table (p.96–112) to determine the encounter.

If two factions claim the same hex, the tile remains neutral.
GATHERING STRENGTH
Factions accumulate Strength by holding and capturing Bases. Each Base a faction controls provides +1 Faction Strength. Your goal is to help the faction you side with hold 3 Bases across the three systems. As a faction gathers power, its influence is felt across the entire sector.
CAPTURING BASES
1. Win a random Starship Fight against the opposing faction to gain access.
2. Once inside, roll a d6 on the Faction Troops table (p.96–112) to find what enemy forces you must defeat. Add +1 to the roll for each Strength point your faction has.
3. Victory: the Base comes under your faction's control.
HYPERLIGHT JUMPS
To travel to a new Star System:
1. Find a Warp Drive and upgrade to HyperLight Drive (300 Scraps at a Settlement Hangar).
2. Gather coordinates by exploring every tile of the map.
3. Spend 10+ Fuel to teleport. Place your ship token on an empty tile and begin the next cycle.
4. Warp between explored systems for 10 Fuel.

Each of the three Star Systems will feature a battle between two or three factions fighting for dominance over the sector. Your objective is to join a faction and help them reach 3 Strength. Use the Strength meter to track each faction's current power.

WHAT IS ASTROPRISMA?
ASTROPRISMA is a sci-fi roleplaying game for 1–5 players about exploring a vast retrofuturistic universe. Create your character, customize your spaceship, gather crewmembers, join factions, and carve your own story as you explore this boundless post-apocalyptic world.
The game is played on a hex grid Star System Map with an Inner Ring, Middle Ring, and Outer Ring surrounding a central STAR hex. You move your ship across the map one hex at a time, discovering planets, factions, and events.
CORE STATS
StatUse
VIGOR (VIG)Strength, endurance, intimidation, brute physical force
GRACE (GRA)Agility, stealth, precision, social charm — also determines TURN ORDER in combat
MIND (MIN)Intelligence, perception, willpower, casting Hacks
TECH (TEC)Engineering, hacking, repairing tech, piloting, deploying drones
THE EXPLORATION CYCLE
STEP 1 — PLAN Declare your intended destination hex on the Star System Map.
STEP 2 — ROLL EXPLORATION Roll d6 on your current ring's Exploration table. Then roll a second d6 to specify the encounter type.
STEP 3 — RESOLVE Play out the encounter — combat, NPC interaction, planet exploration, or a choice.
STEP 4 — END OF CYCLE After resolving: restore 3§ Energy if your HP is at maximum. Advance any time-tracked conditions. Narcobotics wear off. Crewmember Affinity checks if applicable.
MAP STRUCTURE
Inner Ring: Closest to the star. Extreme heat and radiation. Solar Flares and Helios Farms.
Middle Ring: Main play space. Planets of 6 types. Standard encounters.
Outer Ring: Furthest from star. Most dangerous. Space pirates and hostile factions. Pirate Hideouts and Asteroid Seas.
STAR hex: Center of the map. Your starting position when revived from an Abyssal Scar.
CHALLENGE ROLLS — the core mechanic
Roll two d10s. Add your relevant stat to the PLAYER die. Add the opponent's relevant stat (or 0 for environment) to the CHALLENGE die. If the Player die result is STRICTLY HIGHER than the Challenge die result, you succeed.
Example: You try to escape combat (ROLL GRACE). Your GRA is 3. Enemy GRA is 2.
You roll d10 (get 4) + 3 = 7. Enemy's Challenge die: d10 (get 5) + 2 = 7.
Result: 7 vs 7 — NOT strictly higher. FAIL.

You roll again: d10 (get 5) + 3 = 8 vs d10 (get 4) + 2 = 6. SUCCESS.

COMBAT OVERVIEW
1. Turn order: Everyone rolls d10 + GRA stat. Highest goes first. Ties: higher base GRA wins.
2. Your turn: Take 1 Main Action + 1 Side Action. Or swap your Main for a second Side.
3. Enemy turn: Roll d10 on the enemy's move table. Apply the result listed.
4. Hacks: ROLL MIND vs enemy. Success: HACK fires. Fail: roll Malware table (d10).
5. Damage: Roll the weapon's base damage die, add relevant stat. Subtract enemy Armor (♥). Remainder = HP lost.
6. Death: At 0 HP roll d6 + previous scar count. 6+ = dead. Below 6 = Abyssal Scar survival.
PROGRESSION & ADVANCEMENT
EXP: Gained from completing faction missions (+3), certain encounters, and other events. Spend EXP at Wire Docs in Settlements to install Cybertech implants (cost varies by tier).

Cybertech tiers: Tier 1 = 3♥ EXP + 100§. Tier 2 = 6♥ EXP + 200§. Tier 3 = 9♥ EXP + 300§. Max 6 Cybertech implants installed at once.

Hacks: Buy at Medusa Settlements for 150§. Or find as loot. Store in Memory Slots (3 base, up to 6 with Cybertech).

Faction progression: Complete missions to gain Favor. 2 Favor = join faction. 5 Favor = second event. 7 Favor = Warp Drive. 10 Favor = final event and ending.

DEEP SPACE
Texture: The constant low-frequency vibration of the hull under your palms — a faint hum that never fully stops, like pressing your hand against a running engine.
Temperature: The ship runs warm near the reactor compartment, cool near the viewport bulkheads. You learn to navigate by the temperature gradient.
Sound: White noise of recycled air through ducts. A faint tick from the cooling system every 47 seconds. You've counted it without meaning to.
Weight: The zero-g restraint harness digs into your shoulders. Everything in your pockets has a new, unpredictable weight depending on your vector.
SETTLEMENT
Sound: Echoing metal corridors amplify footsteps. Distant machinery — hydraulic clamps releasing, pressurised doors hissing open.
Smell: Engine grease and synthetic protein bars. Cheap fuel residue and something sweet underneath — the recycled air has a faint chemical sweetness.
Texture: Non-slip floor plating with a raised diamond grid. You can feel it through the soles of your boots — uneven, slightly sticky.
Pressure: More crowded than space. People brush past you. The air pressure is tuned differently — a subtle pop in your ears when you dock.
COMBAT
Sound: The crack of a laser discharge leaves a high-pitched ring in your left ear. The thud of physical impact is wet and immediate — nothing like you'd expect.
Temperature: Plasma fire warms the air before you see it. Your cheek stings first. The skin on the back of your hands feels tight.
Weight: Adrenaline makes your hands light and clumsy at the same time. The weapon feels slippery in your grip.
Smell: Ozone from energy discharge. Burnt polymer from melted armour plating. Someone nearby is bleeding.
CYBERSPHERE
Texture: No body, but the impression of one — a phantom outline of yourself at the edge of perception. When you reach for something, your fingers report a smooth, glassy resistance that isn't there.
Sound: Data streams make a tone you can't quite locate — it seems to come from behind your eyes. Corrupted nodes produce a low static that settles in your back teeth.
Temperature: Neutral. Perfectly body-temperature. The uncanny absence of thermal variation is the most unsettling thing about being here.
Pressure: Being Glitched feels like wearing clothes that are slightly the wrong size — everything just slightly misaligned.
PLANETS
Gaian: Fine grit in every joint. The sun bakes the back of your neck. Your lips crack within an hour. Something hard and crystalline under your boots.
Aquarian: Resistance of water even through the pressure suit. Sound travels differently — voices become muddied and directionless. Cold seeping in despite the insulation.
Sylvanian: High humidity makes the air feel thick, almost chewable. Organic smell — wet leaves, decay, something alive and watching. Your boots sink slightly with each step.
Vaporian: The acrid sting of the atmosphere even through the respirator seal. Every breath costs a small effort. The ground is spongy and unpredictable.
Ecumenopolis: Concrete dust coating the back of your throat. Wind channelled between dead towers creates unpredictable pressure changes. Silence of a city that used to be loud.
Calorian: Thermal shock going from ship to surface — the cold hits you before the heat does. Ground that cracks underfoot like glass.
HACKS & CYBERTECH
Casting a HACK: A brief pressure behind your eyes, like a sinus headache about to happen. Then a release — the sensation of something leaving through your temples.
Malware hit: Static in your hearing. Your hands respond a half-second behind your intention for a moment. The taste of copper.
Cybertech activating: Depending on the implant — heat in the arm, a sudden smoothness in your reflexes, or a quiet hum in your jaw you can't localise.
Abyssal Scar: The memory of impact, but no longer attached to pain. Something is different in how your body occupies space. You check your hands without knowing why.
