// TRANSMISSION RECEIVED — CLASSIFIED

The old world
burned clean.

They called it the Collapse. Nobody agrees on the cause — the factions each have their version, and each version conveniently names someone else as the architect of the end. What's certain is this: the great empires that once stretched across star systems are gone, their infrastructure rusting in the outer rings, their populations scattered across salvaged settlements and pirate hollows.

"Untamed Space is what the core calls it.
Out here, we just call it home."

In the wreckage, five factions are fighting to build the next empire — or tear down the idea of empire entirely. W.A.R.G. want liberation through violence. I.S.F. want order through control. Medusa Sector wants the network. Corsair Syndicate wants the profit. Synth Arch wants something older and harder to name.

You are a pilot. Your ship is a salvaged relic, your body is a patchwork of flesh and wire, and somewhere between the outer ring and the galactic core there is a story with your name in it. You just have to survive long enough to reach it.
