✦ LORE — ASTROPRISMA UNIVERSE
POST-APOCALYPTIC UNIVERSE
The light of civilisation has faded. The names of the greatest galactic empires have vanished from common knowledge. Megacities the size of planets are empty and deserted. Titanic spaceships drift lifeless through the void, bearing the scars of past battles.

No one remembers how, when, or why — but the age of humanity came to an abrupt end years, decades, or centuries ago. The few remnants live scattered among a hundred star systems, unable to communicate. All advanced technology was lost. Ships, weapons, and systems in use today are salvaged, rebuilt, and modified to work again.
HOW THE GAME WORKS
Gameplay is segmented into exploration cycles. Each cycle:
1. Move 1 hex — spend 1 Fuel
2. Roll d6 on the ring encounter table
3. Mark your discoveries and write in your journal

Settlements are your oasis in the void — repair your ship, refuel, buy items, install cybertech, and upgrade your starship. Each is run by a different faction.

Your goal: explore the Star System Map and help a faction achieve their final event by reaching 3 Strength.
CORSAIR SYNDICATE
Space pirates, smugglers, and outlaws operating in the outer rings. Respect only strength and profit. Gaining their favour opens black market access. Crossing them means a death warrant.
W.A.R.G. — WESTERN ANARCHIST REVOLUTIONARY GROUP
Insurgent rebels opposing all authoritarian control, particularly the ISF. Guerrilla operations from hidden asteroid bases. Idealistic but brutal when necessary. Offer shelter to proven allies.
MEDUSA SECTOR
Shadowy network of hackers, data brokers, and information merchants. Control the flow of data across the star system. Specialise in cybersphere warfare. Their motives are rarely clear.
INTERSTOLAR FEDERATION (ISF)
Remnants of a once-vast interstellar government. Maintain patrols and key outposts. See themselves as the last hope of civilisation. Others see them as the last gasp of a failed empire.
SYNTH ARCH
Transhumanist movement of synthetic intelligences and cybernetically-enhanced humans. Believe humanity's future lies beyond biological limitation. Creed: reject the material self, nurture the mind, strive for completion.

THE CYBERSPHERE
Once per cycle at a Settlement, spend §3 to log into the Cybersphere through the terminal link.

The network is a circuit of 10 diamond tiles. Choose an Access Port and move 1 tile at a time. Goal: reach Matrix Nodes (valuable data), retrieve rewards, escape through a different Port before time runs out.

You have 10 moves. After 10, memory is erased — logged out and lose §10.

Each unexplored Normal Tile triggers a Cybersphere Encounter when entered.

CAMPAIGN MODE
Spans three Star Systems. Each contains a battle between factions. Join one, help it reach 3 Strength.

To travel to a new Star System: find a Warp Drive, upgrade to Hyperdlight Drive (500 Scraps at a Settlement Hangar).

Difficulty escalates with each system. Third system is all-out war between the strongest factions from previous runs.

Not recommended for a first playthrough — learn the system in Standard Mode first.

HOW TO USE THIS COMPANION
STARTING OUT — Character panel first. Set stats, origin, fill inventory. Set up Starship.

EACH CYCLE — Exploration panel is home base. Select ring, roll Exploration, fill in hex notes. Use Sensory Hook for non-visual scene anchors. AI Narrate for a full scene opener.

ORACLE — Ask Yes/No questions to determine outcomes. Roll Open-Ended for a word prompt when yes/no isn't enough. AI responses calibrated for low visual imagination.

COMBAT — Set enemy name and HP. Roll Challenge for success/failure. Roll Damage on success. Track HP on both sides. At 0 HP roll Abyssal Scar. Roll Loot after victory.

STUCK? — Inspiration panel. Sensory Anchor for scene details. Fiction Prompts for NPCs, objects, places.

AI — Click AI badge to set key. Toggle on in Oracle for AI responses. AI Narrate in Exploration.

SAVING — Auto-saves to browser. Export JSON at end of session. Import on any device.
ORACLE SYSTEM
Roll 2d6. Highest die wins. If equal — No, but…

Both dice low (1-2): add AND. Both high (5-6): add AND. Mixed: plain result.

OPEN-ENDED — Roll 2d6 on the Oracle Word Table. Interpret the word in context. Roll again if it doesn't fit.
