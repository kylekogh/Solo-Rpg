THE EXPLORATION CYCLE — ONE CYCLE = ONE HEX
STEP 1 — MOVE Move your starship to an adjacent hex on the Star Map. Spend 1 Fuel.
STEP 2 — ROLL EXPLORATION Roll on the Exploration table for your current ring (Inner / Middle / Outer). Apply the encounter result.
STEP 3 — PLAY THE ENCOUNTER Resolve what the table gave you — fight, explore, trade, or move on.
STEP 4 — RECORD Write the hex in your journal. Mark it on the Star Map.
STEP 5 — ANOMALY (optional) If using Exp 07, roll the Anomaly Table at the end of each cycle before moving on.
THE THREE RINGS
INNER RING — Easiest encounters. Good for early sessions. Settlements more common.
MIDDLE RING — Balanced risk. Faction presence increases. More valuable loot.
OUTER RING — Hardest encounters. Crime Bosses, ancient wrecks, hidden settlements. Best rewards.
THE ORACLE — ASKING YES/NO QUESTIONS
Any time you need to resolve something the rules don't specify, ask the Oracle a yes/no question.
Roll 2d6 and add them together. Consult the result table in the ORACLE panel.
7 = Yes · 8-9 = Yes, but · 10-12 = Yes, and
5-6 = No · 3-4 = No, but · 2 = No, and
The Oracle Word gives a thematic twist to interpret.
VICTORY CONDITION
Help a faction reach Strength 3 — this triggers their final event and ends that Star System's story. You choose which faction to support by completing their quests and building Favor. The game continues into the next Star System if playing Campaign Mode, or ends as a complete story in Standard Mode.
CAMPAIGN MODE (optional)
Three Star Systems, escalating difficulty. To travel to a new system: find a Warp Drive item, then upgrade your ship to a Hyperdlight Drive at a Settlement Hangar (500 Scraps). Third Star System is an all-out war between the dominant factions from the previous two. Not recommended for your first playthrough.
CHALLENGE ROLLS — THE CORE MECHANIC
When you attempt something risky, make a Challenge Roll:
1 — Choose the relevant stat (VIG, GRA, MIN, or TEC)
2 — Roll a d6 and add your stat value
3 — The GM (that's the game) rolls a Challenge d6 with no modifier
4 — If your total is higher than the challenge die, you succeed. Equal or lower = failure.

NARCOBIOTICS — BOOSTING ROLLS
Vigor Stim or Mind Boost: auto-succeed a roll of the relevant type without rolling. One use. Taking more than one narcobiotic per cycle causes Overdose: −1 MIN until next cycle. Full narcobiotic list in TABLES → NARCOBIOTICS.
COMBAT SEQUENCE
1 — TURN ORDER Both sides roll a die and add their relevant stat. Highest goes first. Ties go simultaneously.
2 — YOUR TURN Take a Main Action (attack, use Hack, use Cybertech) and a Side Action (use narcobiotic, activate some mods, some cybertech abilities).
3 — ENEMY TURN Roll d10 on the enemy's move table. Apply the result to your character.
4 — REPEAT Continue until one side reaches 0 HP.
ATTACKING
Ranged/Melee Attack: Roll the weapon's damage die + the relevant stat.
Hack: Pay the Energy cost (§). Roll MIN vs enemy. On success, the Hack fires. On failure, roll the Malware table.
Cybertech: Most activate as Main or Side actions. Some are passive.
ARMOR reduces incoming damage by 1 per Armor point before applying to HP.
HP, ENERGY & DEATH
HP — Max 20. Reduced by enemy attacks.
Energy (§) — Spent on Hacks and some Cybertech.
Reaching 0 HP — Roll on the Abyssal Scar table (d6 + previous scar count). Results range from recovery to permanent death at 6+.
Between combats — HP and Energy do not automatically restore. Use Vitaplex / Fuelplex narcobiotics or rest at a Settlement.
STATUS CONDITIONS
Overheat — Take d6+MIN damage at start of each turn. Cured by Coolex.
Silence — Cannot use Cybertech or Hacks. Cured by Silencure.
Stun — Cannot act for duration. Cured by Stunbane.
Breach — Exposed. Effects vary by ability. Cured by Breachfix.
Malware — Roll d10 on Malware table when this triggers.
LOOT AFTER COMBAT
Roll d12 on the Loot table, once per difficulty tier:
Easy = 1 die · Medium = 2 dice · Hard = 3 dice · Boss = 3 dice + bonus
Results include weapons, hacks, narcobiotics, armor sets, and master hacks. Full table in TABLES → LOOT.
YOUR STARSHIP
Hull (max 20) — Your ship's HP. Reaching 0 means the ship is destroyed.
Fuel (max 20) — Spend 1 Fuel per hex moved. Refuel at Settlements.
Hyperdrive — 2 charges. Used for special jumps and some Hack abilities.
Critical condition — When Hull reaches 10 or below, your ship's class skill activates.
All ships start with 2 Action Dice except Iron Moth Freighter (3 Action Dice).
SPACE COMBAT SEQUENCE
1 — Roll your Action Dice (2d6 for most ships).
2 — Spend the dice on Weapons (deal damage to enemy ships) and Systems (activate ship abilities like Shields).
3 — Enemy ship rolls d10 on its move table. Apply result.
4 — Shields absorb 3 damage each. Regenerate at end of your turn.
5 — Victory when enemy ship reaches 0 Hull. Roll d6 loot per enemy ship defeated.
FUEL MANAGEMENT
Every hex you move costs 1 Fuel. Some encounters force you to spend extra Fuel (asteroid fields, storms). Running out of Fuel leaves you stranded — you cannot move until you resupply. Fuel is replenished at Settlements and occasionally found as loot or given by friendly NPCs. Monitor it carefully in the outer ring.
SETTLEMENTS — YOUR OASIS
Settlements are controlled by one of the five factions. Your Favor with that faction determines how you're received. High Favor = full access. Low Favor = suspicion or refusal. Negative Favor = hostile response.
WHAT YOU CAN DO AT A SETTLEMENT
Resupply — Buy Health Packs, Energy Cells, Fuel
Buy/sell weapons — Standard weapons always available
Install Cybertech — Implants available for EXP + Serum
Repair Hull — Pay Scraps or Serum
Enter Cybersphere — Costs 3§, grants access to the virtual network
Accept Faction Quests — Build Favor and Strength
Hire Crew — If Favor ≥ 5 with that faction
Ship upgrades — Modules, drives, systems
FACTION FAVOR — HOW IT WORKS
Favor runs from −5 (hostile) to +10 (fully allied). Each faction tracks separately.
+Favor — Complete their quests, help their crew, make choices that benefit them
−Favor — Work against them, complete rival faction quests, attack their ships
≥5 Favor — Can recruit faction crew members
<0 Favor — Hostile on sight at their settlements
Strength (1–3) increases separately by completing major faction objectives. 3 = Victory.
TRADE POST
Each Settlement has a Trade Post where you can buy and sell items. Prices are base values — some Anomaly results and casino effects modify buy/sell prices for a cycle. Faction-specific equipment (e.g. Corsair weapons, ISF gear) is only available at that faction's settlements. The Wanted expansion (Exp 01) unlocks high-power weapons at all settlements once you have Renown 1+.
CHARACTER CREATION
1 — Choose an Origin. This gives you starting stat bonuses and starting equipment.
2 — Note your starting resources: 2 Health Packs, 50 Scraps, and any Origin-specific gear.
3 — Choose your starting Starship class (Smuggler Speeder, Duskwing, or Snowstorm Delta are all free at game start).
4 — Name your character and ship. Begin your first cycle.

LEVELLING UP — EXP
EXP is gained from exploring undiscovered locations, completing quests, and certain Anomaly results.
Spend EXP at Settlements to:
Buy Cybertech — Each implant costs EXP + Serum
Initiate Contracts — 10 EXP to begin a Wanted Contract (Exp 01)
There is no level cap — EXP accumulates and is spent freely.
CREW — HIRING & AFFINITY
Crew members are companions with Active Skills that can assist in combat and exploration. Hired crew: gain 3 Affinity with them to recruit permanently. Faction crew: requires ≥5 Favor with their faction. Affinity increases by helping them, completing their requests, or certain Anomaly results. Max Affinity is 5. Crewmembers with 0 Affinity may leave if treated poorly — the Exo-Anomaly expansion (Exp 07) generates regular crew events based on their emotional state.
HOW EXPANSIONS WORK
Expansions add locations, systems, and mechanics to your base game. Most introduce a new location you place on the Star Map at the specified time — then you can visit it whenever you choose. Exp 07 (Exo-Anomaly) is different: it adds a roll to every cycle and applies from the very first session. Mix and match expansions freely — they're designed to layer.
EXP 01 — WANTED
When: Any time. Spend 10 EXP to activate a contract.
Adds: Renown tracker, bounty hunting loop, high-power weapons (Renown 1+), weapon mods (Renown 2+), 6 named targets with full statblocks.
Rule: Find target via Oracle + deduction → defeat or capture → bring to Settlement for reward + Renown.
EXP 02 — VAPOR SPACE 3000
When: Early game — a virus event transports you.
Adds: Virtual dungeon (3 layers), character class selection, Digital Hearts currency for Neural Hacks, Glitch status system, new enemies including Fairy Titania final boss.
Rule: Move through tiles, fight layer-scaled enemies, reach Matrix Nodes, exit from any SPAWN tile. Die = respawn without Abyssal Scar.
EXP 03 — CLOUD IX CASINO
When: Any time. Mysterious letter invites you. Place on closest outer ring tile.
Adds: Casino infiltration, gambling, high-end narcobotics (including Ambrossia — removes Abyssal Scars), fashion armor, Grand Finale with 3 outcome paths.
Rule: Choose a breaking-in method, explore the casino, acquire Ambrossia, reach backstage for the finale.
EXP 04 — GENESIS
When: Any time. Holographic map signal points to Minimas-V (2 tiles south).
Adds: Cyborg Origin (use at character creation), Experimental Cybertech (unstable implants), Empyrean Laboratories 12-room location, The Prophet NPC.
Rule: Explore the lab freely via corridors (vents need ROLL GRACE). Interact with The Prophet based on your Origin. Discover Project Genesis.
EXP 05 — SEA OF NEON
When: Any time. A digital ad appears in your inbox. Place on any empty tile.
Adds: V-Yen (₩) cryptocurrency, 4 Vestroya Drones (companions with combat abilities), 6 city districts, metro travel, 5 Side Hustles, boss encounter at the Megamall.
Rule: Earn ₩ via Side Hustles or exchange 150§ = 1₩ at Vestroya Spire. Spend ₩ only on Drones.
EXP 06 — ATLANTIS MILLENNIUM
When: Once you've explored all tiles in the Star System. The station warps in.
Adds: 9-sector space station, time anomaly mechanics, Voidcrawler and Xhiroptera enemies, Eldritch Lord of Time (99HP, 9 in all stats) final boss. Late-game content.
Rule: Enter at Sector 01. Move through sectors in order. Sector 09 contains the final confrontation.
EXP 07 — EXO-ANOMALY
When: Every session, from the start. Roll at the end of every cycle.
Adds: Anomaly Roll system — at cycle end, roll d8 for a column (check the column's condition against what happened this cycle — if you don't meet it, reduce by 1 until you find one you do). Then roll d8 for a row to get the Anomaly. Table 1 covers general events.
