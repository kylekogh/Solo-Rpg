BECOMING A RONIN
Your Shinobi story can end in several ways — and some of them continue in the base Ronin game.

Abandoning training: You become a Ronin immediately. Your Master becomes your Final Villain. You are now an enemy of the clan.
Ordered Seppuku: You may attempt to fight your way out — 1d6 Ninjas. Win = escape as a Ronin. Lose = dead.
Captured & tortured (Defeat $/#/@): After 2d6 years, you escape and play as a Ronin. Roll a Scar.
Captured & defects (Defeat %): Continue with the enemy clan as their Shinobi.

When you become a Ronin: Your Nemesis becomes your second Villain in the Ronin game. Carry over your Name, Appearance, Scar, Nightmare, and Compassion/Determination values. Start Ronin Reputation at 0. Open your Ronin companion and continue the saga.
The Nemesis

You have one Nemesis across your entire saga. They can be defeated but never permanently killed — until you face them as a Villain in the Ronin game. Each time they return, they are stronger.

Aphantasia Sense Anchors — Shinobi

The world of Shinobi is tighter and colder than the open road of Ronin. These are felt experiences, not images. Let each one arrive as sensation — no pictures required.

🌑 Moving in Darkness
The discipline of making yourself less than the air. Breath held in the chest, deliberate and shallow. The slight shift in floor temperature between shadow and light — cooler where you want to be. Each footstep placed heel-first, slow, feeling for the creak before committing weight. The sweat of concentration running cold along the spine. A wall at your back: solid, cold, something to know.
🪢 The Training Ground
Stone beneath bare feet, worn smooth by years of other children. The snap of rope against skin during a failed exercise. The smell of the compound: damp wood, straw mats, the iron tang of weapons rarely cleaned. A master's presence behind you — sensed through the displacement of air, the slight drop in sound around him. The burn in the shoulders that means you haven't been dismissed yet.
🗡 Ninjato in Hand
Shorter than a katana — the balance point is different, sits closer to the hand, wants to stab rather than sweep. The grip wrapped in plain cloth, no lacquer, no sound when the sword shifts. The straight blade against the back, not the hip: a different pressure, angular, less comfortable. Drawing it: almost silent, unlike the song of a katana leaving its saya. A weapon that doesn't announce itself.
😶 Wearing a Mask
Cloth tight across the mouth and nose: every breath comes back warmer than it left. The smell of your own body recycled — salt, fabric, the metallic edge of focused fear. Peripheral hearing sharpened — you can hear around corners, but the mask muffles your own voice. The awareness of your face as a shape that could be known — and now, for this moment, isn't. Identity set aside like a sandal at the door.
⛓ Punishment
The cell: stone floor, no soft edges, the cold that accumulates from below. A year measured in the rotation of a single patch of light across the wall you cannot see. Muscles losing their edges. The strange, specific hunger of solitary confinement — not for food but for sound, for another body's warmth. When they finally open the door: the noise of the world arriving all at once, too much, wrong.
👁 Sensing the Nemesis
A room changes when they are in it — something in the pressure of the air, a particular silence that forms around a person who is actively choosing not to make noise. You know the cadence of their breath from memory even if you've never heard it clearly. The wrongness of seeing someone who looks like you, moving differently, choosing differently. Hatred with a face is heavier than hatred without one.
🌒 After the Kill
Hands that don't shake — that's the worst part. The warmth that a body gives up faster than you'd expect. The weight of a scroll or vase or sleeping child: ordinary, mundane, absurdly light given what it cost. The exit route feels longer than the entry. Your own heartbeat audible in the quiet of an emptied room. The mission objective now just an object in your hands. Compassion doesn't argue — it just goes quiet.
🔗 Becoming a Ronin
The compound gate behind you: the sound it makes closing is different when you know you won't hear it open for you again. The road outside has no walls. You have been trained to read confinement as safety — open space is loud in all the wrong ways. Your weapon is the same. Your body is the same. But the name they gave you stays behind with everything else they owned. The road does not care what you were.

Mission Log

What Is Shinobi?

Shinobi is a supplement for Ronin. You play a ninja from the earliest days of training to a possibly tragic end. You need to be familiar with Ronin's core rules — this companion assumes you have the Ronin companion open or available.

Key differences from Ronin: No open road — you play Missions instead. No Villains — instead you have a single Nemesis. Two new interactions: Sneak and Escape. You start with higher Compassion (5) and Determination (5). Age is tracked and increases through punishment and training.

Character Creation

Roll Appearance (optional — may leave blank initially), Family (Shinobi table). You have Ninjatojutsu technique (Fight +1; Block 3). Start with 0 Reputation, 5 Compassion, 5 Determination.

Roll starting Age: 2d6+5. Your character begins as a child. You have a Master — write down their Name and Appearance. The Master is never your Ally.

You begin without a Scar, Nightmare, or Meaning — these are earned through play via punishment and suffering.

The Mission Cycle

Each mission: Introduction → Entry → Execution → Exit → Conclusion.

  • Introduction (Phase 1): Roll to see your morning. Then receive the mission objective (2 dice) and the enemy clan (roll Noble Clan tables from Ronin).
  • Entry (Phase 2): Roll the location type. Determine guards and how to get in.
  • Execution (Phase 3): Roll the Assassinate or Steal table based on your objective. Find your target and deal with what's there.
  • Exit (Phase 4): Face the same guards as Entry (minus any killed). Add extra guards based on your current mission Reputation.
  • Conclusion (Phase 5): Report back. Success = Ninja Training. Failure = Severe Punishment. Any Reputation gained = Light Punishment.
Reputation during missions is bad. Each point gained adds guards on Exit and triggers Light Punishment in Conclusion. Keep it at zero.

New Interactions: Sneak & Escape

Sneak (pass unnoticed): Roll Yin vs Yang. Add Determination to Yang. Add Reputation to dark Yin (or +2 if it's an animal). Yang > Yin = pass unnoticed. Fail = +1 Reputation and you must face the opponent. Multiple enemies: one roll, but add each extra enemy to dark die.

Escape (flee combat): No dice. Spend 1 Determination per enemy to avoid combat. Gain 1 Reputation per enemy left behind.

The Nemesis

You have exactly one Nemesis across your saga. They are your rival. Create them with Name, Appearance, and Technique. Roll the Nemesis Connection table.

Each time you defeat the Nemesis, gain +1 Determination or +1 Compassion. Even if you kill them, they may return — next time stronger (roll Nemesis Returns table) and bearing a scar from your last encounter.

The Nemesis can only be permanently defeated after they become a Villain in your Ronin saga and you kill them there. If you transition to Ronin, your Nemesis becomes your second Villain.

Punishments & Age

Severe Punishment is earned by failing a mission or on the worst light punishment rolls. Usually involves years of torture, a new Scar, and aging.

Light Punishment is earned for every point of Reputation gained during a mission. Involves imprisonment or forced labour — years pass, Reputation resets, sometimes a new Scar.

Over 30 years old: Every Light Punishment must be followed immediately by a Severe Punishment. Age becomes a liability.

Allies in Shinobi

If you make a teammate an Ally, they become a Fighter with the Ninjato technique (Fight +1; Block 2 — one less Block than the standard). Your Master is never an Ally. Ninja techniques cannot be taught to Mentor-type allies.

Becoming a Ronin

Several paths lead out of the clan and into the base Ronin game:

  • Abandon training: You become a Ronin. Master = Final Villain. You are an enemy of the clan.
  • Seppuku ordered: Fight 1d6 Ninjas. Win = escape as Ronin. Lose = dead.
  • Captured & tortured (Defeat $/#/@): 2d6 years pass. You escape. Roll a Scar. Play as Ronin.
  • Captured & defects (Defeat %): Join enemy clan. Continue as Shinobi.
When transitioning to Ronin: carry Name, Appearance, Scar, Nightmare, Compassion, Determination. Start Reputation at 0. Your Nemesis becomes Villain 2. Your Master (if Final Villain) becomes Villain 3. Open your Ronin companion and continue.
