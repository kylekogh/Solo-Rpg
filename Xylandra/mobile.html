Xylandra — The Lost Planet
A savage world of ancient alien ruins, exotic civilizations, planetary warlords, airships, lost technologies, and primal conflict. Once a thriving nexus of cosmic power, now fractured by forgotten wars, temporal distortions, and the struggle between its many species.
A World Out of Time
Xylandra is lost in a temporal anomaly, drifting between past, present, and unknown futures. Some civilizations have existed for millennia; others arrived unexpectedly, pulled from distant worlds or eras. The sky holds fragments of a shattered moon in unstable orbits. Ancient ruins pulse with forgotten power, some activating at unpredictable intervals.
What Is Xylandra?
Some believe it is a prison world. Others claim it is a test. A few insist it is a tomb. Some say it is an experiment gone awry — and that the experiment is still running.
History is fragmented. Different cultures interpret events differently. No single account is authoritative.
??? — Era of the Builders
A great advanced civilization ruled Xylandra. They constructed massive cities, created bioengineered life, harnessed the planet's energies. Something happened. A war. A rebellion. A cosmic disaster. Their machines and temples still function — automated, patient, waiting.
Unknown — The Fracturing
The sky split and time became unstable. New species appeared from other worlds, from Xylandra's own past and future. The planet became a battleground. No one knows how long this period lasted. Time was unreliable.
Recent Past — The Warlord Age
Old empires collapsed. Sky-pirates, raiders, and warlords fought over remaining stable territories. Some rediscovered forgotten technologies. Others turned to brute force.
Now — Age of Savage Blades
The era you play in. Bone-Kings consolidate power. Freeholds resist from above. Rift Scholars dig too deep. The Cult guards its ruins. Ironfang sells its blade. The Ghosts drift between moments. Your story begins here.
Bone-Kings of the Wastes
Warlord Tyrants · Frailty: Bound by the Code of Strength
Self-proclaimed rulers of the wastelands. Only the strongest can lead. Fortress-cities rise from the corpses of conquered nations. Loyalty earned in battle. Goal: rule all Xylandra through war.
Skyborne Freeholds
Free Traders & Rebels · No Unified Command
Loose coalition of skyship captains, rebels, relic-hunters. No empire — only shared principles. Trade in information, salvage, and passage. Will fight fiercely to keep the air free.
Rift Scholars
Forbidden Knowledge Seekers · Obsession Overrides Morality
Secretive order studying the unstable forces fracturing Xylandra's reality. Believe mastering the anomalies could reshape or save the world. Many disappear into the Riftlands and emerge as something else entirely.
Cult of the Builder-Gods
Fanatical Zealots · Blind Faith
Worship Builder tech as sacred. Restore and guard ruins. Trespassers dealt with harshly. A Cyber Templar incorporating Builder tech is either ultimate devotion or profound blasphemy — depends on who you ask.
Ironfang Mercenary Guild
Elite Warband for Hire · Loyalty Only to Coin
Loyal to no king, no cause — only the highest bidder. Once a deal is struck, it is absolute. Have slaughtered former allies when the contract demanded it. To cross them is to invite death.
Ghosts of the Fracture
Riftborn Outcasts · Unstuck from Time
So thoroughly exposed to the Rift they no longer belong to any single moment. Drift between times. Know things they shouldn't — events that haven't happened, people not yet born. Feared by most, desperately sought by some.

The Shattered Expanse
Vast broken land of floating ruins, gravity distortions, Builder relics. Once the seat of a great empire, now left to wander time itself. Entire lost civilizations appear overnight and vanish by morning.
The Warlord's Wastes
Crimson dunes, fortress-cities, eternal conflict. Bone-Kings clash for dominance over forgotten technology buried beneath the sand. The heat is lethal. The politics are worse.
The Bioluminescent Jungles
Ancient forests where plant life glows, pulsing with energy. Predators use the light to hunt. Ruins almost entirely reclaimed by growth. Navigation without a guide is nearly impossible. Guides rarely return from the deep jungle.
The Riftlands
Territories where temporal anomalies are so dense that time itself is unreliable. When you emerge, an unknown amount of time may have passed — or you may emerge before you left. The Ghosts call it home. Everyone else avoids it.
The Skyways & Floating Ruins
Fragments of a shattered moon drift in unstable orbits, along with sections of ancient cities flung skyward during the Fracturing. Freeholds navigate these territories. Skyship combat here is complicated by shifting gravity and unpredictable debris.
The Crystalline Spires
Towers of natural or Builder-grown crystal rising from the desert floor. They resonate with energy — electronics behave strangely nearby, relics activate without warning. The Cult considers them sacred. No one sleeps near them willingly.
The Warlord Clans
Rule through strength and ancient law. Tech-infused weapons, relic-forged armor. Status displayed through scarification and embedded metal sigils. A warlord's heraldry is as much armour as their plate.
The Skybound Nomads
Some have never touched the ground. The ship is home, family, and identity. Skyborne Bards sing epic ballads using crystal amplifiers to carry voices across vast distances.
The Sandborn Tribes
Masters of beast-taming, scavenging, and surviving the deadliest landscapes. Know routes through the sands that appear on no map. Have outlasted every empire that tried to conscript or destroy them.
The Riftborn
Touched unstable energies, gaining unnatural abilities or fragmented memories of past lives. Some glimpse moments that haven't happened. Some exist in two times simultaneously. Most are shunned. A few are desperately sought.
The Machine-Kin
Descendants of cybernetic warlords and forgotten experiments. The line between biological and mechanical has blurred across generations. A Cyber Templar belongs to this lineage whether they claim it or not.
Art & Culture
Theater of the Rift — actors step into time anomalies to re-enact historical battles in real-time. The audience never knows if it's performance or genuine temporal bleed.

Artifact Crafters — can reassemble lost technology into functional weapons and tools. A skilled crafter can name their price anywhere on Xylandra.
Magic is not fully understood. Some believe it is a science too complex for the mortal mind. The distinction between technology and sorcery has been meaningless since the Builders disappeared.
Builder-Tech Sorcery
Use of ancient artifacts and long-lost sciences. Capable of altering matter, creating shields, forging energy weapons. Most practitioners activate devices without understanding the principles. True mastery is almost unheard of.
Rift-Touched Abilities
Surviving a rift anomaly sometimes produces teleportation, temporal sight, or the ability to exist briefly outside normal time. These powers are not controllable. May go months dormant then manifest violently under stress.
Warlord Blood Rites
Ancient combat rituals predating the Builders' arrival. Warriors who complete them become harder to kill through no measurable biological change. The Cult ignores it. The Scholars want to study it. The warriors don't care either way.
Using Magic in Play
Handled through the oracle like everything else. Ask whether the relic activates, the ability manifests, the rite holds. Yes, and… means it works and something unexpected happens. No, and… means it fails and something gets worse.
Sandstrider Dune-Worm
Burrowing Colossus
Devours entire caravans. Blind — senses by vibration. Do not run. Do not stomp. Do not drop anything heavy.
Void-Touched Harbinger
Riftborn Abomination
Warps reality. Absorbs energy. Fades if unfed. Drawn to Builder relics specifically. The Rift Scholars attempt to control it.
The Forgotten Stalker
Shapeshifting Lurker
Mimics its prey perfectly. Cannot maintain form for long — watch for faces that slip at the edges. Replaces those it consumes.
Howling Nightfiend
Shadowstalker
Cannot exist in direct sunlight. Emits mind-breaking screams. Thrives on terror. Keep a light source. Keep company. Keep moving.
The Thing Beneath
Nightmare Unknown
Exists outside of time. Can speak to the dead. Bound to a single location. It waits. It is always waiting.
The Last Sky-Titan
Extinct God-Beast
Controls the weather. Flight without limit. Cannot regenerate — every wound is permanent. Believes itself to be the last guardian of the world. Cannot be killed without consequence.

How To Use This App
Starting out — Character panel first, then Adventure Hook for your opening situation.

During play — Scene panel is home base. Fill in location and objective. Oracle for questions. Twist Counter auto-tracks doubles.

Stuck? — Inspiration in the More panel. Sensory Anchor for concrete scene details. Fiction Prompts for NPCs and objects.

Conflict — Set opponent Luck before starting. Alternate attacks. At zero Luck: mortal danger.

AI features — Tap the AI badge top-right to set your API key. Toggle on in Oracle for AI responses. AI buttons in Scene and NPC panels.

Saving — Auto-saves to this browser. Export JSON at session end as backup.
