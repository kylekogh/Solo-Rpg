After Your Rest — Choose a Table
When prompted after longer rests, choose from one of these tables. Each result asks you to CHANGE SOMETHING.
MUCK AND MURK — TARNISHED METAL — SLOW, INEVITABLE EROSION
1Gold and silver dull. Iron rusts. Colours fade to a sepulchral grey. What would your creator say if they saw you now?
2A colony of small creatures nests close to where you rest. You become obsessed with their routines. Describe them. Do you see their downfall or are you recovered?
3By frost, corrosion or something stranger, a prominent setting or rune is damaged irreparably. How does the damage affect one of your properties?
4The yawning silence is deafening and you struggle to keep your mind calm. Can you stay composed or does something snap?
5A vital part deteriorates and no amount of restoration can return you to your former utility. How does this fundamentally change your primary purpose?
6A sapling grows around you and, with time, becomes a mighty, gnarled tree. How does your magic shape the tree, reflecting your inner nature?
THE SUN TURNS — EMPIRES RISE AND FALL — HEROES FADE TO MEMORY
1Time reframes the actions of one of your Keepers, once considered a selfless hero, as immoral or mercenary. What happened and what actions are twisted or reinterpreted?
2You are collected and catalogued by a municipal institution. Through excessive bureaucracy you are lost in their arcane system. Where do you eventually emerge?
3You are obtained by a travelling merchant ignorant of your true power. What is the strangest place you visit as they make their extensive circuits?
4A descendant of one of your favourite Keepers grows into a twisted mirror of their ancestor. Who are they and what do they do?
5You are swallowed by a passing sentient ooze — translucent and hungry. Where does the creature make its home, and what else churns with you in its viscous form?
6Your name passes into common speech as a phrase, with a meaning somehow connected to your history. What is the expression and what does it come to mean?
VALIANT ACTS — VANQUISHED EVIL — INSPIRING MANOEUVRES
1A stronghold of great importance was attacked by a dreadful, monstrous horde. How did you help your Keeper drive off the fearsome assailants?
2Your Keeper defeated their sworn, bitter rival in a duel. Where did the contest take place and why was their animosity so severe? How did you help them win?
3A great warrior was trapped in an impossibly tall tower by an evil sorcerer. Why did your Keeper owe them their life, and how did you help free them?
4A long-standing feud between two prominent factions was resolved. How did you help your Keeper convince the fractious leaders to negotiate?
5A mythical beast stood watch over an ancient treasure. With strength or trickery — and your help — your Keeper overcame them. What were they protecting?
6Your Keeper uncovered an insidious conspiracy in their people's ruling class. With your help they exposed the scheme. What was the price for speaking truth to power?
TREACHEROUS ACTS — TRAGIC LOSS — DEMEANING MISUSE
1You were locked in a vault while poorly-made counterfeits were sold to hoodwinked adventurers. What fault in the design made these copies fail at a crucial moment?
2You were thrown carelessly atop a heap of treasure. There was another sentient item within the hoard. What did you learn from this other intelligence?
3You were put on display to decorate a wall. You rested there for a long time, unused. What was the room like? Were you kept clean or did the dust pile up?
4Powerful magic has a toll. You corrupted your Keeper, causing malaise, sickness and, eventually, death. Was this ruination intentional?
5You intentionally deceived or refused to help your Keeper, leading to their demise. What took place and why did you act in that way?
6You were broken, split or torn into two pieces by your Keeper, intentionally or otherwise. How did this happen and how were you made whole again?

How to Play
You are an immortal magical item. You cannot act alone — you need a Keeper to wield you.

Setup: Choose your Artefact type, describe your creation, add 3 Traits. Draw your item.

Each Keeper: 1. Describe the Keeper and how they acquired you · 2. Answer their two questions · 3. Choose Victories & Valour or Neglect & Mischief (then CHANGE SOMETHING) · 4. Answer one Artefact Question · 5. How do they lose you? · 6. Time & Rest

Acts: 3 acts, 2 Keepers per act (minimum). Act I = Newly Forged. Act II = A Time of Glory. Act III = The Ruination.

Time & Rest: Between Keepers, choose how much time passes and rest for the corresponding duration. Longer rests prompt table choices.

CHANGE SOMETHING: When prompted, add/remove/change a Trait, physical detail, Artefact Question answer, or world detail.

Ending: When 10 minutes of Rest have passed across the whole game, or your story is ready to end, your next Keeper is your last. End with an epilogue — sit in silence and think about how your item is remembered.
What You Need
An hour or two · A quiet, ideally dark place · A journal or paper to write and draw on · Art materials · A way to play the soundtrack (theartefact.now.sh) · A way to track time (this app handles it)
Safety Note
As an intentionally solitary, somewhat melancholy experience, Artefact may not be the best thing to play when struggling with feelings of loneliness. If the game leaves you feeling isolated, consider taking a break. Mind (bit.ly/mind-loneliness) has support options.
