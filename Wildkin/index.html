⬡ Flow of Play
Each Act — 8 Steps
1. Create your Wildkin (first time only). Choose a home Location.
2. Choose the Region for this act (home region for Act 1; different for 2 and 3).
3. Choose a Trainer. Describe them and answer their three questions.
4. Choose and answer a Reflection Question.
5. Choose and answer a Change Question, then Change.
6. Act 1 or 2: Imagine the final moment with this trainer. How did they lose you?
7. Wait in your ball. Go to the Timer panel. Choose how long to wait.
8. Act 3 only: Your last trainer. They release you — or lock you away forever.
When Asked to Create a Wildkin
1. Choose an Element from your current Region
2. Choose a Body Type (roll or pick)
3. Describe what they are like
4. Give them an impressive battle ability
5. Give them a name
When Asked to Create a Location
1. Choose an Element from your current Region
2. Choose a Location Type (roll or pick)
3. Describe what the location is like
4. Consider marking it on your map
When Asked to Change
Change something about your Wildkin. Consider: adding an ability or weakness · updating your description · modifying a previous answer. You may change multiple things at once. You may also Change whenever it fits your story, even if not explicitly prompted.
Remember
You are kept in a ball. Captured Wildkin are stored in ball-like capsules, locked away from the world. You were once free.

Play safe. This game may touch on loneliness or confinement. Answer what you are comfortable with. Take breaks when needed.
